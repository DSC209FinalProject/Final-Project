Project Summary

Have you ever wondered how much world famous artists such as The Weeknd actually affect the music industry and its trends? We noticed that this question is sparsely explored in existing visualizations on music, which motivated us to create a more interconnected and comprehensive way of analyzing this topic.

We have divided up our visualizations into 3 unique sections, all of which are interconnected and provide a piece of the story. We recommend to view the sections in the order as they appear. However, feel free to explore in however many ways you like! There is also a guide at the bottom of the page if you need a hint.

1. Seasonal Overview: When are Top Hits Released?

Start your explorations here, discover the distribution of Spotify hit songs throughout 2023. Click on any month to see detailed statistics and audio features. Click on "Select Months" to compare two month slices at a time, select "Show Average" to compare the month slice to the 2023 average.

Click a slice to explore that month.

2. Temporal Overview: How Did Audio Features Change Over Time in 2023?

This graph reveals how average audio features evolve throughout the year. Check or uncheck different audio features to separate them out. It might be a good idea to reference this graph again after viewing Section 3!

3. Artist Impact: Do Top Artists Actually Dictate Trends?

Top artists might dominate streaming numbers, but do they actually define the music landscape? Click on any artist to compare their preferred audio features against the 2023 average. By taking the artist's most preferred features and comparing it to Section 2's temporal graph, could you tell if their music affected the music landscape?

Need a Hint?

Start with the pie chart, select a month you would like to explore. Note down the top performing artist of that month, and find the artist in Section 3. Note down their top features and compare those features against Section 2's line graph. Did the respective audio feature shift in relation to the artist's feature? If it did or did not, that should tell us a piece of the story on whether the top performing song affected the song trends of that month.
