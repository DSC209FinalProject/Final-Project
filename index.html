Project Summary

Have you ever wondered how much world famous artists such as The Weeknd actually affect the music industry and its trends? We noticed that this question is sparsely explored in existing visualizations on music, which motivated us to create a more interconnected and comprehensive way of analyzing this topic.

We have divided up our visualizations into 3 unique sections, all of which are interconnected and provide a piece of the story. We have a recommended exploration combination below, which presents the insight to our question when viewed in that order. However, feel free to explore in however many ways you like!
